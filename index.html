An AI eye on California's protected waters.

Upload a coastal photo and get a structured poaching-probability assessment in seconds. Built on Google Gemini 2.5 Flash. Every output is a recommendation for a human ranger to review — never an automated citation.

What this can't do

    
    
      
  • False alarms. Research vessels, kayakers, and rescue boats can all look suspicious in a still photo. Treat every output as one frame's worth of evidence — not a verdict.
    • 
      
  • Blind weather. Fog, glare, rain, and night blind the camera. The AI can't replace human surveillance during the conditions a poacher would choose on purpose.
    • 
      
  • Privacy spillover. Photos go to Google's servers. Don't upload images of identifiable people, license plates, or anything that wasn't meant to be on camera.
    • 
      
  • Boundary blur. A boat fishing legally just outside a reserve looks identical to one inside it. The model can't see the line on the water.
